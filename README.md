# Welcome!

Welcome to my collection of publicly available scripts!

This repository is where I'll be sharing scripts and projects for public use, with everything being **open-source wherever possible**. I believe in transparency and making my work accessible to everyone.

While I intend to make as much of my work publicly available as I can, some scripts may remain private due to personal reasons. I appreciate your understanding.

### Transparency & Trust

I understand that trust takes time to build, especially within a growing community. By making my work open-source, I hope to demonstrate my intentions, provide insight into what my scripts do, and build credibility through transparency.

I want everyone to have the opportunity to explore, review, learn from, and use my publicly released work.

### Thank You

I genuinely appreciate everyone who takes the time to visit this repository, explore my projects, or make use of the scripts I share.

Your support means a lot, and I look forward to sharing more of my work with you.
